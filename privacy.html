Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Athos History uses a small private tool to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. The tool is used only by the channel's owner.

What it accesses

The tool signs in to the Athos History YouTube account with the owner's permission and uses that access only to upload the channel's own videos and to read the channel's own list of uploads. It does not access any other person's account or data.

What it collects

Nothing. The tool does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information about viewers or anyone else. The sign-in token is kept only on the owner's own computer.

YouTube and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the tool can be removed at any time at Google account permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Athos History YouTube channel.
